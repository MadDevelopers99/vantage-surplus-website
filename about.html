Our Story

From a Local Buyer to a Regional Surplus Network

Vantage Surplus Traders was founded in Riyadh with a simple idea: businesses shouldn't have to struggle to turn unwanted inventory into cash. What began as a local trading desk has grown into a regional network with buying relationships across Saudi Arabia, the GCC and 25+ countries worldwide.

Brand protection is our first priority — as a reputable liquidation partner, we handle every transaction with care for your company's identity and reputation. We specialize in turning surplus assets, obsolete inventory, dead stock and unused material into valuable resources, and our meticulous approach ensures every item is assessed and handled with the utmost care.

  • Fully licensed trading entity registered in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia
  • Dedicated evaluation specialists for every stock category
  • In-house logistics for free nationwide & GCC collection
  • We pay 5–30% of your stock's original cost, plus free shipping & labour
