Privacy Policy

How Vantage Surplus Traders collects, uses and protects your information.

Last updated: August 18, 2026

1. Introduction

Vantage Surplus Traders ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us through this website, our forms, WhatsApp, phone or email. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

2. Information We Collect

  • Contact details you submit, such as name, company name, phone/WhatsApp number and email address
  • Details about the stock you wish to sell, including category, quantity, condition and location
  • Technical information such as browser type, device and general usage data collected automatically when you visit our site

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to evaluation requests and provide stock offers
  • To arrange collection, logistics and payment once an offer is accepted
  • To send occasional market updates or newsletters, only if you opt in
  • To improve our website and services

4. How We Protect Your Information

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the personal information we hold from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction. Access to your information is limited to team members who need it to complete your request.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties. Information may be shared with logistics partners strictly to arrange collection of purchased stock, or where required by law.

6. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information at any time by contacting us at the details on our Contact page. You may also unsubscribe from marketing communications at any time.

7. Cookies

This website may use basic cookies or local storage to remember your preferences and improve browsing experience. You can disable cookies through your browser settings.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

9. Contact Us

Questions about this policy can be sent to hello@vantagesurplus.com.
