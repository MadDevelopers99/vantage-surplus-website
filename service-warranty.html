Service & Warranty

What's covered when you buy or sell through Saudi Overstock Buyers.

Last updated: August 18, 2026

1. Evaluation Service

Every stock evaluation is carried out by our specialist team, free of charge, within 48 hours of receiving complete stock details. Evaluations are based on the information, photos and access provided by the seller at the time of submission.

2. Collection Service

Once an offer is accepted, collection is arranged and carried out by our own logistics team, free of charge, within Saudi Arabia and the wider GCC. We aim to schedule collection within 2–5 business days of offer acceptance, subject to site accessibility.

3. Condition of Purchased Goods

Items listed on our Buy marketplace are sold as surplus or overstock inventory in the condition described at the point of listing. Where an item is marked "new" or "sealed," this reflects the seller's declared condition at intake and is inspected where practical before relisting.

4. Manufacturer Warranties

Where surplus stock is still covered by an original manufacturer's warranty, that warranty (if any) remains with the manufacturer and is unaffected by resale through our marketplace. We recommend buyers confirm warranty status directly with the manufacturer for electronics, machinery and tools.

5. Our Service Guarantee

  • Transparent, no-obligation pricing on every evaluation
  • No hidden fees for evaluation, loading or collection
  • A dedicated point of contact for every transaction

6. Contact Us

For service or warranty questions, email s.overstockbuyers@gmail.com or reach us via WhatsApp.
